# Phantom revision 6

This revision addresses the side-profile problems in the traced model: chest depth, lower-leg shape, blade construction, and head placement.

Construction guide: generated with the built-in image generation tool as a design reference. It is not a render of the Blender mesh. The actual Blender renders are identified separately.

Open **Phantom_Revision6.blend** for the editable scene. The supplied original reference images and the new AI construction guide are packed inside it. Enable the REFERENCE collections to view them.

Files beginning with **blender_** are actual renders of the saved mesh. **construction_guide.png** is the AI design reference generated with the built-in image generation tool; its exact prompt is in **construction_prompt.txt**.

Changes in this revision:

- Replaced the pointed chest with one shallow chassis and flat layered armor.
- Replaced the inflated calves with tapered, beveled mechanical shells and separate shallow access panels.
- Rebuilt the helmet with narrow emissive sensor strips, a vented two-facet mask, temple vents, and a visible connected neck.
- Rebuilt both blades with a straight spine, fixed diamond cross-section, and carriers independent of the fists.
- Rebuilt the shoulder cannons with shorter stepped shrouds, elevation mounts, and recessed muzzle cavities.

The retained shoulders, hips, arms and hands come from the preceding model. Thigh outlines are retained with reduced depth to match the new lower legs. This is a revision of the 3D asset; fine detailing remains an approximation of the concept artwork.
