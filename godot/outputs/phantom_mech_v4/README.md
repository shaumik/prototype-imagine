# PHANTOM — assembly and proportion revision 4

Open **Phantom_Mecha_v4.blend** in Blender. This is the editable 3D model, with separate components and inspection cameras.

This revision rebuilds the hands as enclosed fists in a neutral stance, with palms toward the thighs and thumbs folded forward across the curled fingers. The blades deploy from independent housings attached to the outer forearms, with clearance beside the fists. The helmet is smaller, has recessed flat sensor slits, and sits above a visible neck with rotary bearings and piston supports. The shoulder cannons have continuous layered armor casings and dark, recessed four-bore muzzles. The broad body armor panels have planar faces and small edge bevels to remove uneven domed highlights.
